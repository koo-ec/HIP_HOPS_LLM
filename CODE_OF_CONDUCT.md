# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the HIP-LLM project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- taking responsibility, apologising and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the project and wider community.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, public or private intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behaviour is inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer through a verified institutional or GitHub contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.
